I fell in love with glass in an intro course my sophomore year and haven't left the hot shop since. My practice is sculpture-led, often pairing glass with metal and wood. I work across many processes: furnace blowing and blown molds, steel, wood, sand and kiln casting, lost-wax choosing the method that best serves the idea. Today I'm focused on bespoke, sustainability-minded pieces that bridge sculpture and design: objects with presence, purpose, and a lighter footprint.
