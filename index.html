I'm Julia Majchrzak,

A multidisciplinary designer, artist, and product thinker interested in the intersection of creative innovation, material exploration, and strategic business development.

My experience spans the product lifecycle, from design and development to buying and merchandising. I am particularly interested in how creative intuition, consumer behavior, and commercial strategy come together to shape meaningful products and experiences.

I graduated from Tulane University in May 2026 with a degree in Business, minor in Design, and an independent focus in glassblowing. My interdisciplinary background drives my curiosity to explore new mediums, challenge conventional processes, and discover connections between seemingly unrelated fields.

My 9 to 5: I'm currently working at Gap Inc. in San Francisco, supporting multiple product categories at Banana Republic Factory. Through my experience across design, buying, and merchandising, I've developed a holistic perspective on the product lifecycle and an interest in bridging creative development with consumer insights and business strategy.

My 5 to 9: I'm a glassblower exploring the intersection of art and function through lighting, furniture, jewelry, and experimental objects. I'm also the co-founder of Juma, a glass jewelry brand focused on sustainability and versatility, and I never see you., an art and technology collective exploring new forms of creative expression.
