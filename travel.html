Travel has been one of my greatest teachers as a designer and artist. Experiencing different cultures—their art, textures, colors, and traditions—has given me a global perspective that continually shapes my creative process. Whether it’s the architecture of a city, the patterns in local textiles, or the way communities express themselves through fashion, I draw inspiration from each place I visit. These influences have pushed me to think beyond boundaries, blending diverse mediums and aesthetics into my own work. Looking ahead, I aspire to keep traveling as an essential part of my career—broadening my horizons, discovering new sources of creativity, and weaving cultural stories into everything I design.
